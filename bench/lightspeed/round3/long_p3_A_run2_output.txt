The interplay between transformation and validation is critical; errors in transformation often trigger validation failures, leading to bottlenecks or data loss.

**Storage** serves as the repository for processed data. The log references storage in the context of "throughput," "latency," and "errors," suggesting that storage systems are not static endpoints but dynamic components that must handle high write loads and read requests.
